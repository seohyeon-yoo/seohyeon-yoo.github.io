About Me

I am Seohyeon Yoo, an incoming M.S. student in AI Systems at KAIST, advised by Prof. Sung-Ju Lee. I received my B.S. in Data Science from Hanyang University and studied as an exchange student at Universidad Carlos III de Madrid.

My research focuses on digital healthcare, human-computer interaction, and human-centered AI. I study how behavioral and physiological data and conversational systems can support people during vulnerable moments in everyday life.

Previously, I was a research intern at the KAIST Mobile Intelligence & Interaction Lab and Hanyang Data Mining & Algorithm Laboratory, and an engineering intern at Samsung Electronics DS AI Center.

✦Research Interests: Digital Healthcare, Human-Computer Interaction, Human-Centered AI
