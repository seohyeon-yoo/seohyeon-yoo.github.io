About Me

I am Seohyeon Yoo, an incoming M.S. student in AI Systems at KAIST, joining the Mobile Intelligence & Interaction Lab in September 2026 under the supervision of Prof. Sung-Ju Lee.

My research interests lie at the intersection of digital healthcare, human-computer interaction, and human-centered AI. I study how behavioral and physiological data can help detect vulnerable moments in everyday life, and how conversational systems can provide timely and meaningful support.

I am currently investigating digital interventions for eating disorder recovery, including early detection of binge eating episodes and recovered-peer support mediated by LLM-based chatbots.

Before starting my master’s, I worked as a research intern at KAIST MIIL, where I studied digital interventions and conversational support for eating disorder recovery. I also worked as an engineering intern at Samsung Electronics DS AI Center, developing an anomaly detection pipeline for semiconductor process data and an LLM-based proposal review agent. Previously, at Hanyang Data Mining & Algorithm Laboratory, I investigated debiased retrieval-augmented sequential recommendation.

✦Research Interests: Digital Healthcare, Human-Computer Interaction, Human-Centered AI
